# Design Story

## Theme

"Built, not decorated." The page reads like an engineer's changelog turned into an editorial
spread: monospace metadata, version-like section numbers, and a single lime accent that behaves
like a cursor highlight in a code editor.

## Emotional journey

1. **Arrival — confidence.** A short curtain lifts and the name rises letter by letter in huge
   type. A soft lime glow follows the pointer: the page is alive and responds to you.
2. **Momentum — energy.** A two-row skills marquee speeds up and reverses with your scroll
   velocity, so scrolling itself feels physical.
3. **Understanding — calm.** The About statement lights up word by word as you read it; numbers
   count up to give a quick sense of scale.
4. **Proof — trust.** The experience timeline draws itself as you move through it. Skills pop in
   by category.
5. **Peak — delight.** Projects arrive as sticky stacked cards: each new card slides over the last,
   which recedes and dims. Images unmask and tilt in 3D under the pointer.
6. **Resolution — action.** A giant closing line and a magnetic "Get in touch" button that pulls
   toward the cursor make the next step obvious.

## Restraint

One accent color, two typefaces, and one "peak" moment (stacked projects). Everything else is
supporting motion that finishes in under ~1s and never blocks reading.
